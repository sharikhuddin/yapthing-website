More questions & answers
Can I use YapThing directly in my browser?

Yes. Open YapThing Chat on the web without installing an app. Some features require a signed-in account or additional permissions.

Does YapThing support Android and iPhone?

Android is in closed testing, and the native iPhone app is in development. Public app-store access is not yet available to everyone. The web chat works in supported mobile browsers.

Can YapThing help with writing, research and projects?

Yes. The web workspace includes conversation, writing, research support and projects. Live sources, connected tools and Work actions depend on provider availability and usage limits.

How does Incognito Chat work?

Incognito conversations are not saved to YapThing chat history. They still require AI processing by the underlying provider, and service logs or provider retention policies may apply.
