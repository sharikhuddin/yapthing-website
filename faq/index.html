Questions? Start here.

Find straightforward information about YapThing, its features, plans, availability and limitations.

What is YapThing?

YapThing is an independent AI assistant for conversations, writing, research, studying and project planning. The web workspace opens in a browser.

Can I use YapThing without installing an app?

Yes. Open the web chat in your browser. Some tools require an account, provider authorization or a paired computer.

Does YapThing always provide current or accurate answers?

No. AI answers can be wrong. Live research depends on provider availability and quota. Review linked original sources and verify important claims.

Is YapThing available on Android and iPhone?

Android is being prepared for closed testing and public release. The iOS version is in development. Testing invitations and public downloads are not available to everyone. Contact support for current access.

Are paid plans available?

The plans page lists Free access and proposed Plus and Pro plans. Paid checkout and benefits remain subject to rollout verification. Check the plans page for current terms.

How does computer Work operate?

Computer Work requires an online paired computer and authorized providers. Review the proposed actions and credit cost before approval. Third-party costs are separate.

Is YapThing medical advice?

No. Medical content is for education. Verify it against primary materials and seek qualified clinical advice for personal medical decisions.

Where can I find privacy and deletion information?

Read the privacy policy and account deletion page. Support can help with questions about access and data.
