About YapThing

Technology should help ideas feel more possible.

An independent AI assistant designed to bring writing, research, learning and everyday work into one approachable space.

YapThing is an independent AI assistant for conversations, writing, research, study and project planning. This website provides official product information and links to the web workspace.

What is available?

Web chat is accessible in a browser. Features depend on accounts, providers and usage limits. Mobile release status is described on the product facts page; do not infer public app-store availability from a development or testing milestone.

Content standards

  • Separate available features from previews and planned releases.
  • Describe provider requirements and known limitations.
  • Use visible content that agrees with structured data.
  • Do not present unverified ratings, customer counts or search rankings as facts.

AI answers and evidence

AI output may be wrong or incomplete. Source links must be checked against the original material. Educational content does not replace professional judgment.

Corrections and support

If a product claim is inaccurate, contact support with the page URL and the wording that needs correction. Do not send passwords, access tokens or private customer records.
