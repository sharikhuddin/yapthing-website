What can connected tools help with?

Review important Gmail or Outlook messages, draft replies, and confirm before sending.

💻 Work on projects

🔄 Keep your setup together

Use one YapThing account to manage your connections across the website and mobile apps once platform sync is enabled.

Availability: Public research tools can work without an account connection. Private plugins require a verified provider authorization and enabled YapThing integration; selecting a plugin does not grant access. Features described above are intended workflows, not a claim that every integration is active.

How does YapThing protect my accounts?

You authorize access through the provider's official sign-in process. YapThing should request only necessary permissions, protect tokens server-side, and let you disconnect. Sensitive actions, such as sending email or deleting files, should require your approval. A disconnected provider must not be shown as connected.
