Project drafts reserve 5 credits while running, then settle at 1 credit per 2,000 weighted tokens (input + twice output), rounded up and capped at 5. Confirmed failures release the reservation. This allowance measures completed work rather than elapsed time; your review time is free.

When are credits used?

Approval reserves the displayed credits. Completion settles them. A confirmed failure releases the reservation. An interrupted task keeps its reservation until its result is checked, preventing duplicate actions and charges. Reads and previews are free.

Do credits follow my account?

Signed-in Work credits share one server balance across supported YapThing clients. They reset on the first day of each month at 00:00 UTC and do not roll over. Guest local prototypes and regular local chat keep their device counters.

Are AI and plugin subscriptions included?

Code drafts use your installed local Ollama model. GitHub CLI and Desktop Commander use your own authorized accounts. Any third-party plan, hosting, model download or computer running cost is separate. This preview does not start paid cloud inference.

What happens when I run out?

Your saved work remains available. You can continue free reads and reviews, wait for the monthly reset, or check paid plan availability. Prepaid credit packs can extend eligible chat and research after the included daily allowance. Secure web checkout must be available before a purchase can be completed.
