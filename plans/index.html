How do daily limits reset?

Daily allowances are intended to reset at 00:00 UTC. Monthly allowances follow the applicable subscription cycle. Actual counters and enforcement are still undergoing testing.

Can I upgrade from Plus to Pro?

Yes, once your billing provider confirms the upgrade. Your existing account and conversations remain the same. Proration depends on the payment platform.

Can I use the same plan on mobile and web?

Plans are associated with the same signed-in YapThing account; cross-platform payment and entitlement synchronization must be verified before relying on this benefit.

Web purchases use Stripe Checkout. Android and iOS in-app purchases use their respective app stores and RevenueCat. Subscription access is shared through the same YapThing account after provider confirmation.
