Product overview

Meet YapThing. A space for your ideas.

Learn what YapThing offers today, and what features and app releases are still being prepared.

YapThing is an independent AI assistant for conversations, writing, research, study and project planning.

What can I do?

  • Draft and revise messages, outlines and documents.
  • Explore questions with research tools and inspect linked sources when available.
  • Study concepts and organize project plans.
  • Review computer tasks through an authorized, online paired computer.

Where can I use it?

Start with web chat in a browser. Android testing and iOS development are ongoing; public mobile downloads are not available to everyone. Contact support for current access.

What are the limits?

AI answers may be inaccurate. Live research depends on provider quota and availability. Computer Work requires pairing and authorization. Paid plans and benefits remain subject to rollout verification.
