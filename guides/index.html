Quick answers about YapThing

Concise answers, with links to the relevant product details and limitations.

How do I check whether an AI research answer is accurate?

Read the linked original sources, compare dates, and verify important facts independently. A source snippet does not prove that an entire page supports a claim. See the research guide.

Do I need an account to use YapThing?

You can open YapThing Web Chat in a browser. Certain features, saved conversations and usage allowances require a signed-in account.

What is the difference between Chat and Projects?

Chat is for a conversation. Projects help group related conversations and work. Explore the product overview for current availability.

What happens when I choose Incognito?

Incognito conversations are not saved to YapThing's chat history. AI providers still process requests, and service logs or provider retention may apply. Review privacy information.

What can YapWork do?

YapWork can help plan or review tasks on an authorized, connected computer. Actual actions depend on available tools, user permissions and usage limits. See YapWork details.

Are all YapThing answers current?

No. Live research depends on provider availability and quotas. Check original sources for decisions that require recent or verified information. See the FAQ.
